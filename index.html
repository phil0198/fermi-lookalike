      How to play

      Three questions.
      One guess each.

      Every answer is a number. You won't know it exactly, and that's the point. Break the problem into pieces you do know and multiply your way to an estimate.

      Each guess is scored by how many times off it was. Guessing 50 when the answer is 100 is 2× too low, the same as guessing 200 is 2× too high.

      Your daily score combines all three (a geometric mean). Lower is better, and 1.00× is perfect.

      Shorthand and math both work: 2.5k, 40m, 1.2b, 365*24.
